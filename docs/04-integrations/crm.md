# CRM Integration

## Boundary
CRM: contacts, conversations, meetings, pipeline, sales, revenue and relationship.
Growth OS: media delivery, creatives, experiments, operational work and performance.

## CRM → Growth
Examples:
- communication.created
- meeting.processed
- sale.created

Growth OS stores references/projections needed for growth decisions rather than cloning the CRM.

## Growth → CRM
Examples:
- report.completed
- account.risk_detected
- relationship-information-requested

## Identity
Growth clients should map to CRM organization/contact identifiers through explicit integration mapping records.
