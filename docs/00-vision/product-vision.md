# Product Vision

Growth OS is the operating layer for running paid media and growth delivery with AI.

## Problem
Paid media operations are fragmented across ad managers, analytics, spreadsheets, creative folders, task tools, chats and human memory. This limits the number of accounts an operator can manage with consistent quality.

## Vision
Create a system that continuously turns business context and performance data into analysis, decisions, operational actions, experiments and reusable learning.

## Primary user
Initially: the internal paid media operator / Althos Growth operation.

## North-star operational question
How many client accounts can one skilled operator manage with high quality when assisted by Growth OS?

## Not the goal
Growth OS is not initially a public SaaS, generic marketing chatbot or replacement for the CRM.
