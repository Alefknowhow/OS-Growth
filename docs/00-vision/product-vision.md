# Product Vision

Growth OS is the operating system of an automated paid traffic and acquisition agency.

## Problem
Paid media delivery is fragmented across ad managers, analytics, spreadsheets, creative folders, editing tools, chats and human memory. Each account consumes operator hours on repetitive analysis, small account edits, creative production and reporting. This caps how many clients one operator can serve with consistent quality.

## Vision
A system that continuously turns client context, raw media and performance data into analysis, plans, executed actions, creatives, reports and reusable learning — with the operator acting as controller, not as executor.

The operator:
- defines policies and autonomy limits per client;
- reviews plans and approves what policy requires (click or voice);
- gets briefed (daily digest, voice summary) on what was analyzed, executed and learned;
- intervenes on exceptions.

Growth OS:
- syncs and normalizes ad data, detects signals, analyzes;
- drafts plans (budget, status, structure, creative rotation) and executes them through policy-governed tools;
- produces creatives from the client's media library using pre-built creative skills (Higgsfield, motion graphics, copy);
- generates reports and publishes them to the client portal;
- learns from outcomes and experiments.

## Primary users
1. Agency operator / owner (control seat).
2. Internal team members (media buyer, creative) as the agency grows.
3. Clients, through a restricted portal (metrics, reports, creative approvals, media upload).

## North-star metric
**Clients managed per operator at target quality**, supported by: operator minutes per client per week, share of actions executed without manual work, plan approval latency, first-pass creative approval rate and client KPI attainment.

## Not the goal
- Not a CRM (Auto CRM owns sales and relationship).
- Not a generic marketing chatbot.
- Not unrestricted AI access to ad accounts: autonomy is earned per client and per action type through policy and track record.
- Not a public SaaS initially (internal-first, multi-tenant by design).
