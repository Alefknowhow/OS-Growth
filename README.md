# Growth OS

AI-powered operating system for paid media and growth operations.

Growth OS is an internal-first platform designed to centralize paid media, creative operations, experimentation, analytics, projects, and AI agents in a single operational environment.

## Vision

Build an intelligent operating layer that helps a media buyer manage more accounts with greater consistency, context, speed, and operational quality.

Growth OS combines:

- Paid Media
- Creative Operations
- Performance Analytics
- Experimentation
- Projects & Tasks
- Client Intelligence
- AI Agents
- Automated Workflows
- Reporting
- CRM & Revenue Intelligence

## Core Principle

**Data → Analysis → Decision → Action → Experiment → Learning**

Growth OS is not an ads dashboard with an AI chat attached. It is an operating system for executing and continuously improving growth operations.

## Product Boundary

Growth OS is intentionally separated from the CRM.

- **CRM:** source of truth for contacts, conversations, meetings, opportunities, sales, revenue, and customer relationships.
- **Growth OS:** source of truth for paid media delivery, creatives, experiments, operational tasks, performance, and growth learnings.
- **Integration:** API, webhooks, and event-driven workflows connect both systems without duplicating their core responsibilities.

## Initial Scope

The first version is for internal use. The priority is operational leverage and learning, not SaaS commercialization.

Initial AI team:

1. Growth Orchestrator
2. Performance Analyst
3. Creative Strategist
4. Content Agent

Autonomy will be introduced progressively through explicit policies, approvals, audit logs, and bounded actions.
